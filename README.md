Sushi Rush is a fast-paced casual arcade game in which players keep a sushi conveyor belt running smoothly while serving hungry piggy customers before they lose patience. Players tap sushi buttons to place dishes onto the conveyor belt, then time their choices so the correct sushi reaches the matching customer. As levels progress, more sushi varieties, faster conveyor speeds, shorter time limits, and special golden customers increase the challenge. Players must also prevent the conveyor belt from overflowing while using boosters and earned coins strategically to complete each stage.

Core Gameplay Loop
Customers arrive and request a specific sushi dish.
Players tap the corresponding sushi button to place that dish onto the conveyor belt.
Sushi automatically travels around the conveyor.
When the correct sushi reaches a waiting customer, it is served automatically.
Serve all required customers before the timer expires while avoiding conveyor belt overflow.
Key Mechanics
6 sushi varieties are gradually introduced as players progress.
Increasing difficulty through:
More customers per level
Additional sushi types
Faster conveyor belt
Reduced level timer
Golden customers with lower patience
Boosters
Fast Belt (temporarily speeds up the conveyor)
Clear One (removes an unwanted sushi from the belt)
Economy
Earn coins by completing levels.
Spend coins on boosters, extra time, and sushi restocks in later levels.
Failure Conditions
Conveyor belt becomes full.
Time runs out before serving all customers.
